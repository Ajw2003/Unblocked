Unblock design walkthrough · Feature 1 of 6 · Pull request #9

Blockers

Most tasks don't stall because of laziness. Something small is in the way, it can't be fixed right then, so the task gets put off and drops out of sight. Blockers make that small thing visible and turn it into the next thing you can actually do.

The loop it breaks

Today, without blockers
Dishes need doing→ No soap→ Put it off→ Out of sight, forgotten→ Sink overflows
…and the app keeps dealing you "Do the dishes", which you still can't do.
With blockers
Dishes need doing→ Name it: "Buy dish soap"→ The soap is what gets dealt→ Soap done→ Dishes come back, ready
The thing in the way never leaves your view, because it is a task in its own right.

Try it

This is a working model of what pull request #9 does, with a small made-up deck. Nothing here touches your real Unblock.

  1. Projects: tap Do the dishes, type Buy dish soap under "What's in the way?", tap Save.
  2. Focus: tap Chore a few times. The dishes never come up.
  3. Projects: open the dishes, tap Focus on this. The note tells you what to do first.
  4. Tick off the soap and watch the dishes come back.
Unblock

What Unblock is tracking

Updates as you use the model. A blocker is just another task; the blocked task remembers which tasks it waits on.

    Choices I made for you

    These are the calls I made without asking. Any of them can change before you merge.

    A blocker is a full task

    What I did
    "Buy dish soap" becomes a real task, so it can be dealt, ticked off, synced and put on GitHub like any other.
    The other way
    A short note on the task ("needs soap") that is only a reminder and is never dealt.

    Same folder, same labels

    What I did
    The soap goes in Home and gets the Chore label, so asking for a chore can deal it.
    The other way
    Ask where each blocker goes, or send them all to an "Errands" folder or the inbox to sort later.

    Blocked tasks are never dealt

    What I did
    While anything is in the way, the dishes are left out of the deal and the card counts.
    The other way
    Deal them anyway, with the blocker shown on the note.

    Blockers clear themselves

    What I did
    Tick off or delete the soap and the dishes are ready again. There is nothing to unlink by hand.
    The other way
    Ask "Is the dishes task ready now?" when a blocker is done.

    Blockers can be blocked

    What I did
    The soap can have its own blocker ("Get paid Friday"). The chain unwinds one step at a time.
    The other way
    Only one level, to keep it simple.

    Not built yet

    • The GitHub issue doesn't mention blockers, so on GitHub the dishes look ready.
    • The Board screen doesn't show blockers.
    • You can't remove a blocker without ticking it off or deleting it.
    • Blockers don't change how heavy a task feels. That comes with the energy feature, next.
