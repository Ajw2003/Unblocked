Unblock design · Blockers, round 3 · Your direction

Fold and suggest

Each task stays one line. Folded, it shows how much is cleared and one suggested next step. Unfolded, it shows every blocker in order, and you can tick any of them, reorder them, or pick a different one to do next. The suggestion saves you choosing, but it never stops you.

Try it

Things to try

  • Tick the suggested step straight from the folded task.
  • Tap Not now: the suggestion moves on, and stays off that blocker until the others have had a turn. Nothing is reordered for good.
  • Tap a task name to unfold. Tick any blocker, in any order.
  • Use ↑ ↓ to change the order. The top open blocker is what gets suggested.
  • Add a blocker from the unfolded task.
  • Tick a whole task while blockers are still open. It works; Unblock clears the rest and offers Undo.

Questions left

  1. When you ask for a chore and the dishes are blocked, what should the deal hand you? (a) skip the dishes, (b) "Do the dishes, start with: Buy dish soap", or (c) just "Buy dish soap" on its own. I lean to (b): you see the task and the small thing you can do right now.
  2. Should a "Not now" be remembered until tomorrow, so the same suggestion doesn't come straight back? Or only until you close the app?
  3. Steps and blockers: the app already has "steps" on a task. Should they become this same list, so there's one list of "what this task needs", or stay separate?
