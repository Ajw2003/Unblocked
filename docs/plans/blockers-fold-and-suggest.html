Unblock design · Steps, round 4 · Your answers built in

Fold and suggest

Each task stays one line. Its steps fold under it, with one suggested next step showing. When you ask for something to do, Unblock hands you that step and tells you which tasks it moves forward. Skip anything, in any order; how long a skip lasts is up to you.

Things to try

One step, several tasks

  • Projects → unfold Mop the floor, type soap. Unblock offers the soap step the dishes already have. Tap it instead of making a duplicate.

The deal hands you the step

  • Focus → Chore. You get a step, not a blocked task, with the tasks it helps listed under it.
  • Deal until you get Buy dish soap: it now helps the dishes and mopping. Tick it and both move forward.

Not now, remembered

  • Tap Not now on a step. It shows as skipped, with Bring back, so it's out of the way but not gone.
  • Settings → choose how long a skip lasts. Close the app there shows the default at work.

Ticking a whole task

  • Tick a task with steps still open. Its own steps finish with it; a step another task needs stays open for that one, and Undo puts it all back.

What changes in the real app

  • Steps replace blockers. The app's old steps (one per line, hidden in the editor) become this list. The task's existing steps carry over as its first steps.
  • GitHub sees them. Steps already go into the issue as a checklist, so the gap from the first build closes.
  • Pull request #9 gets reworked to this design before you test it: no blocker rows in the list.
  • One thing to decide later: should a step that turns out to be big (three steps of its own) be able to grow into a task? Not built; noted for when it happens.
